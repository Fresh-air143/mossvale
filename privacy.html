Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

Summary

Mossvale collects nothing. There is no Mossvale server, no account system, no analytics, and no telemetry. Everything happens on your machine and on the platforms you connect to.

What is stored, and where

  • Platform credentials (e.g. a TikTok access token) are stored only on your own computer, in your local configuration directory, with file permissions restricted to your user. They are never sent anywhere except directly to the platform that issued them.
  • Generated videos and metadata (provenance records such as seeds and file hashes) remain in local files on your computer.

What leaves your machine

Only what you publish: the video files you choose to upload go directly from your machine to the platform you selected (for example, TikTok’s Content Posting API). Mossvale operates no intermediary service and sees no copy of that traffic.

Third parties

When you connect a platform such as TikTok, that platform receives your authorization and processes your uploads under its own privacy policy. This site (a static GitHub Pages page) sets no cookies and loads no trackers.
