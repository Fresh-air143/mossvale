Mossvale

A desktop studio for self-generated simulation shorts.

Mossvale turns deterministic, seeded physics simulations — ball races, overflow builds, and wave grids — into short-form videos, then schedules and tracks them across the creator’s own social accounts.

With TikTok, Mossvale uploads a finished video as a draft into your own TikTok account, so you can review and post it yourself. It accesses only your own account, never posts publicly without your action, and never interacts with other users or their content.

Local first. Mossvale runs on your machine. Your credentials and tokens stay on your machine. There is no Mossvale server, no account system, and no telemetry.
